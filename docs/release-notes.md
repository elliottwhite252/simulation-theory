# Release notes

## 0.1.0: First playable prototype

The first version you can play from start to finish.

### New

- **A complete level.** Walk through the neon city, clear two enemy rooms, and defeat a boss.
- **Boss fight: the Broadcast Van.** OmniCast's armored truck can only be damaged from behind and attacks faster when it's below half health. See [The boss fight](concepts/boss-fight.md).
- **Opening cutscene.** A three-panel story intro that sets up Iris and OmniCast. Press **Esc** to skip it.
- **Live synthwave soundtrack.** The music is generated in the browser as you play, with no audio files. Press **M** to mute it.
- **Retro 16-bit look.** The game now renders at 480 × 270 and scales up with crisp pixels, in the style of SNES and Genesis games.

### Known issues

- Some small text is hard to read at the new resolution. A pixel font is planned.
- Neon sign glows look thicker than intended.
- There are no controller or touchscreen controls yet.
